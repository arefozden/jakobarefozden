NDLA topic reference
LESSON 01

Personal data and GDPR

Understand

Personal data relates to an identified or identifiable person. GDPR requires lawful, fair and transparent processing, purpose limitation, minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation and security. Consent is one possible lawful basis, not the only one.

Try it yourself

Inventory fictional data collected by a school event registration form.

Knowledge check: Should you collect data just in case it becomes useful?

No, collect only what is necessary for the purpose.

LESSON 02

Rights and responsibilities

Understand

Controllers determine purposes and means; processors act on their behalf. Individuals have rights including access and correction, and in certain conditions deletion or objection. Special-category data has extra protection.

Try it yourself

Explain who controls data in a fictional registration service.

Knowledge check: Is deletion an unconditional right?

No, exceptions and legal obligations can apply.

LESSON 03

Tracking and cookies

Understand

Cookies store information in a browser; some support login while others support tracking. Fingerprinting combines device characteristics. Tracking protection, privacy-focused search and extensions can reduce tracking; VPNs do not make users anonymous.

Try it yourself

Compare browser privacy settings without sharing personal browsing history.

Knowledge check: Does private browsing stop all tracking?

No.

LESSON 04

Publishing and privacy by design

Understand

Plan privacy before collecting or publishing information. Use fictional data, restrict access, define retention and review permissions. Check applicable rules before sharing identifiable pictures or recordings.

Try it yourself

Redesign a registration form to collect less data.

Knowledge check: What is data minimisation?

Collecting only the data needed for a defined purpose.
