Analog and Digital

Analogt og digitalt · VG1 IM

Learning objectives

  • Explain digitalisation using a practical example.

Analog signals

A microphone converts air pressure variations into a continuously varying electrical signal. Amplitude describes signal strength; frequency describes cycles per second.

Digital signals and sampling

Sampling measures a signal at discrete times. Quantisation rounds each value to one of a finite number of levels. More samples and levels can improve fidelity but require more data.

Binary and data units

Bits represent two states. Eight bits make one byte. Decimal units use powers of 1000; binary units such as KiB use powers of 1024.

Digitalisation

Digitisation converts information into digital form. Digitalisation changes how work is organised using digital technology. Scanning a form is digitisation; redesigning the workflow is digitalisation.
