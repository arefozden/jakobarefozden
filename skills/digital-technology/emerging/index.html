LESSON 01

VR AR and MR

Understand

VR replaces much of the visible environment with a simulated world. AR overlays digital content on the real world; mixed reality integrates virtual objects with spatial context. Tracking, latency and accessibility affect experience.

Try it yourself

Sketch an AR museum guide and a VR training activity.

Knowledge check: Which technology overlays information on the real world?

AR.

LESSON 02

AI and chatbots

Understand

Machine learning models learn patterns from training data. Generative AI predicts and produces content, which may be incorrect or biased. Chatbots can use rules or models; evaluate outputs and avoid entering personal information.

Try it yourself

Design a rule-based school FAQ chatbot using fictional data.

Knowledge check: Why verify AI output?

It may contain plausible but false claims.

LESSON 03

IoT and sensors

Understand

Connected devices collect measurements using sensors and can control actuators. A smart greenhouse may measure temperature and trigger ventilation. Security, updates, power and connectivity matter.

Try it yourself

Draw sensor → controller → actuator for a greenhouse.

Knowledge check: What does an actuator do?

Performs a physical action.

LESSON 04

Ethical evaluation

Understand

New technologies should be evaluated for usefulness, accessibility, privacy, energy use and unintended effects. A prototype is a way to test assumptions before investing heavily.

Try it yourself

Compare two technologies for a school use case.

Knowledge check: Why test with different users?

To identify differing needs and barriers.
