LESSON 01

Student safety

Understand

A safe learning environment includes physical and psychosocial conditions. Report hazards, bullying and unsafe equipment to responsible staff. Keep pathways clear and follow school procedures.

Try it yourself

Inspect a classroom with teacher permission and propose improvements.

Knowledge check: Who should you tell about a damaged electrical cable?

Responsible staff; do not use the equipment.

LESSON 02

Ergonomics

Understand

Adjust chair, screen and keyboard to support comfortable posture. Reduce glare, vary tasks and take movement breaks. Headphones should be used at comfortable volume.

Try it yourself

Create an ergonomic workstation checklist.

Knowledge check: Why vary posture?

To reduce prolonged static strain.

LESSON 03

Workplace HMS

Understand

Systematic safety work identifies hazards, assesses risk, implements measures and reviews results. Employers and workers have different responsibilities under applicable Norwegian rules. Report near misses as well as accidents.

Try it yourself

Assess a fictional cable trip hazard with a risk matrix.

Knowledge check: What is a near miss?

An event that could have caused harm but did not.

LESSON 04

Environment and equipment

Understand

Extend equipment life through maintenance, repair and responsible reuse. Electronic waste and batteries require appropriate collection. Securely erase data before approved disposal.

Try it yourself

Design a responsible retirement plan for a school PC.

Knowledge check: Why not put batteries in ordinary waste?

Fire and environmental risks.
